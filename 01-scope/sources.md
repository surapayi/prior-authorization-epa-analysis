# Sources

Every number in this project comes from one of these.

| Source | What I used it for | Year | Scope and limits | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CMS Interoperability and Prior Authorization final rule (CMS-0057-F), fact sheet | Decision timeframes (72 hours expedited, 7 calendar days standard), FHIR API requirement, which plans are covered | Rule finalized 2024; timeframes from 2026, APIs from 2027 | Applies to specific payer types. Excludes drugs. | https://www.cms.gov/files/document/fact-sheet-cms-interoperability-and-prior-authorization-final-rule-cms-0057-f.pdf |
| AMA Prior Authorization Physician Survey (2025 edition) | PAs per physician per week (40) and weekly hours spent (13, physician and staff time) | 2025 | Physician-reported. Covers all PA types, including drugs. | https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/prior-authorization |
| AMA Prior Authorization Physician Survey (2018 edition) | Share of physicians waiting 1, 3, and 5+ business days for a decision | 2018 | Physician-reported and old. Check the 2025 report for an update. | https://www.ama-assn.org/practice-management/prior-authorization |
| CAQH Index 2023, provider specialty issue brief | Minutes and cost per manual PA by specialty (specialists about 26 minutes, $15.12 manual vs $6.61 electronic) | 2023 | National. Follows CAQH's own definition of a transaction. | https://www.caqh.org/hubfs/CAQH%20Insights_2023%20Index%20Report_Provider%20Specialty%20Issue%20Brief_Final.pdf |
| CAQH Index 2023, overall averages ($10.97 manual, $5.79 electronic) | Cost cross-check | 2023 | I found this figure in a secondary article. Confirm it in the CAQH Index report. | https://index.caqh.org |
| Washington Office of the Insurance Commissioner, annual prior authorization report | Payer decision time (8.3 hours, standard, outpatient medical/surgical) and MRI request volumes | Plan year 2024 (published 2026) | Commercial fully insured plans only. Carriers report top codes, so MRI may be missing for some. Hours may be rounded. | https://www.insurance.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-03/2026-prior-authorization-report_0.pdf |
| Washington Health Plan Prior Authorization Data (open data portal) | MRI-specific decision times: filter by CPT codes such as 73721, 72148, 70553 | Ongoing | Same limits as the OIC report | https://data.wa.gov |
| Aetna Medicare Advantage 2025 prior authorization metrics report | Payer decision time range (mean ~0.9 to 3.1 days, median 0 to 1 day, standard requests) | 2025 | One payer, Medicare Advantage only, by contract number. No MRI split. No state label. | https://www.aetna.com/content/dam/aetna/pdfs/wwwaetnamedicarecomSSL/individual/website/2025_PA_Metric_Report.pdf |
| BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS), medical secretaries and administrative assistants (SOC 43-6013) | Hourly labor rate: Seattle-area ~$29.01, US median ~$22.08 | May 2025 | I took the annual figures from secondary sites and divided by 2,080 hours. Confirm on bls.gov. Wages exclude benefits. | https://www.bls.gov/oes/ |
| HL7 Da Vinci implementation guides: CRD, DTR, PAS | Future-state design | Current releases | Technical guides. Read for concepts. | https://hl7.org/fhir/us/davinci-crd/ , https://hl7.org/fhir/us/davinci-dtr/ , https://hl7.org/fhir/us/davinci-pas/ |
