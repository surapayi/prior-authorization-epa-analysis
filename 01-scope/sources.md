# Sources

Every number in this project comes from one of these.

| Source | What I used it for | Year | Scope and limits | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CMS Interoperability and Prior Authorization final rule (CMS-0057-F), fact sheet | Decision timeframes, FHIR API requirement, covered plans | Final 2024; effective 2026–2027 | Applies to specific payer types. | Washington Office of the Insurance Commissioner, annual prior authorization report | MRI request volumes | Plan year 2024 (published 2026) | Commercial fully insured plans only. Carriers report top codes, so MRI may be missing for some. | [insurance.wa.gov](https://www.insurance.wa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-03/2026-prior-authorization-report_0.pdf) |
| AMA Prior Authorization Physician Survey (2025 edition) | Staff dedicated to PA (40%), PA delaying care (95%) | 2025 | Physician-reported. | BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (OEWS) | National hourly wage: mean $22.50 (same occupation) | May 2025 | Wages exclude benefits. | [bls.gov](https://data.bls.gov/oes/#/area/0000000/2025) |
| HL7 Da Vinci implementation guides: CRD, DTR, PAS | Future-state design | Current releases | Technical guides. Read for concepts. | [CRD](https://hl7.org/fhir/us/davinci-crd/), [DTR](https://hl7.org/fhir/us/davinci-dtr/), [PAS](https://hl7.org/fhir/us/davinci-pas/) |
