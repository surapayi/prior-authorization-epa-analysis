## KPI definitions

| KPI | Definition | Formula |
|---|---|---|
| Turnaround time | Calendar time from order created to response received. Includes the payer's wait. | T2 − T0 |
| Clinic staff effort per PA | Active staff minutes on the Clinic staff lane steps. Waiting time is not counted. | Prep and submission minutes + (follow-up attempts × minutes per attempt) + minutes to log the decision |
| Cost per PA (clinic side) | Cost of that staff effort. | Staff effort minutes ÷ 60 × hourly labor rate |
| First-pass rate (optional) | Share of PA requests the payer accepts as complete on the first submission, with no return for missing information. | Requests accepted on the first try ÷ all requests |

| KPI | Washington / Seattle-area (main) | Nationwide (cross-check) | Notes |

| First-pass rate | No reliable baseline | No reliable baseline | 5 |

## Notes

1. **Turnaround:** no published figure for the full turnaround. Prep time and the delay before staff see the response are assumptions. For a benchmark, the CMS rule sets 7 calendar days for standard decisions for covered payers.
2. **Staff minutes:** the ~26 minutes is the CAQH Index 2023 figure for specialists. It is used as a single value to keep the table simple.
3. **Wages:** BLS OEWS median for medical secretaries and administrative assistants. Seattle-Tacoma-Bellevue about $60,350 per year, US about $45,930 per year, each divided by 2,080 hours. Figures were taken from secondary sites, so confirm on bls.gov. Wages exclude benefits. If you add a benefits load, state the percentage.
4. **CAQH costs:** national, provider-side cost per manual transaction, using CAQH's own definition. They will not match the minutes × wage formula exactly.
5. **Assumptions:** first-pass rate has no reliable baseline, so leave it blank until you find a source or clinic data. The number of follow-up attempts and the minutes per attempt (used in the staff effort formula) also have no source. Label these clearly and show a low, base, and high case.
