# As-Is Baseline KPIs

Reference values for the manual PA process. These are published estimates, not measurements from a real clinic. KPI definitions and formulas are in the [scope statement](../01-scope/scope-statement.md).

| KPI | Washington-based (main) | National (cross-check) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turnaround time (T0 to T2) | No data | No data | 1 |
| Clinic staff effort per PA | About 26 min | About 26 min | 2 |
| Cost per PA (clinic side) | $28.20/hr × 26 min = **$12.22** | $22.50/hr × 26 min = **$9.75** | 3, 4 |

CAQH cross-check for cost: $15.12 per manual PA for specialists (CAQH Index 2023). Washington has no separate figure.

## Notes

1. **Turnaround.** Found no published figure for the full time from order created to response received. The CMS rule sets 7 calendar days for standard decisions for covered payers, but that is a deadline for the payer's decision only. It is not a baseline for T2 − T0, so this number is to show for context only.
2. **Staff minutes.** The 26 minutes is the average that specialists reported for a manual PA in the 2023 CAQH Index (2022 data). It is a self-reported total per transaction. CAQH does not say which steps it includes, so it may not match this project's "active staff time" definition. 
3. **Wages.** Hourly wage for medical Secretaries and Administrator, used as a stand-in for the clinic staff who prepare, submit, and log PA requests. Washington: mean of $28.20/hr, from the Washington State Employment Security Department OEWS data (May 2025). National: mean of $22.50/hr, from BLS OEWS (May 2025). Wages exclude benefits. The role is an assumption, since clinics assign PA work to different staff.
4. **CAQH cost.** CAQH reports provider-side cost per manual transaction using its own method. It will not match minutes × wage exactly. The gap is expected, since CAQH is likely to include costs beyond base wages.
